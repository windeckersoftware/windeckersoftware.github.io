Shoresh is a reading app for language learners. Tap a word, see it in your own language, grade it, and keep going. Books, articles, and YouTube live in the same vocabulary.

What it does

◐

Read

EPUB, TXT, and PDF, plus pasted text and web pages. Page colour, type size, contents, and bookmarks. Chinese and Cantonese split into words, not single characters.

Aa

Grade, then review

New words are blue, learning words amber. A grade of 1 comes back in 10 minutes. Grades 2–4 come back over days. Known and skipped words stay out.

▶

Watch

The video sits on top. Under it, captions fill the rest of the screen in your learning language, with a translation in your base language. Tap a word for its meaning in that same sheet.

ש

Only three YouTube controls

A YouTube mark opens the YouTube home. A search field runs a search. The channel name opens the channel you are watching. Recommendations, comments, and the bottom bar stay out of the way.

◉

Your languages

You choose what to learn, what to translate into, and the interface language. A Hungarian base and an English video means the definition is Hungarian, not English and not Hebrew.

Aa

Highlights, optional

Caption words are coloured by how well you know them. The Aa button on the caption sheet turns that colouring off, and back on.

⌕

25 dictionaries, on the phone

Hebrew through Thai, including Chinese, Japanese, and Korean. If a word is missing, Shoresh asks an online translation once and keeps the answer.

◒

Stays on the device

No account and no Shoresh server. Export a backup, or an Anki file, when you want a copy.

⇄

Two phones, same Wi-Fi

One phone hosts, the other joins with an address and a short code. Words and progress merge. Book files stay on the phone that imported them.

Install

Download the APK on your Android phone.

Open it from the notification or Files. Android will ask permission to install from this browser — allow it once.

Read. No account, no sign-in. Your words stay on the phone.

Android will warn that the app is from an unknown developer, because it is not on the Play Store. Allow the install once. If you already have the earlier Ner build, this replaces it and keeps your words. The name on the phone is שורש.
